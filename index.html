How to be a basement rat (aka, an instrumental major)

Presented by a basement rat

Step 1. Get an instrument

There are many instruments to choose from and you can always try as many as you want
LaGuardia welcomes many classes to learn said instruments and there are many bands and orchestras to join

Our bands are concert band, symphonic band, and wind symphony
Our orchestras is symphony orchestra and the philharmonic orchestra
There are many other string orchestras that depend on age and grade

Step 2. Learn how to navigate the basement

Since the basement is split between both the drama and instrumental majors, it could be difficult to find all the rooms

Yet, LaGuardia as the A sections such as rooms like A27 or A17
A07 and A13 are the practice rooms
A15, A17, A27, and A29 are band and orchestra rooms
All other rooms are either teacher rooms or classrooms for theory classrooms

Step 3. Choosing what you want to play

So what music do you want to play?
Well, there is a lot of types of music that you can try
There are the regular bands that you can try or you can try doing orchestra
There is also jazz if you are interested in jazz studies
If you ever want to play in a opera, operetta is a way to engage in opera

Step 4: Actually having fun

Remember that playing an instrument is a lot more than practice and playing
You get a lot of concert experiences with all of them being really fun
You get to meet other musicans and have a lot of time to hang out with others
It really is an exciting time to express yourself through music
